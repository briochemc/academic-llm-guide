# GenAI tools for reading and writing papers

Prices are USD per month for one person, as shown on each vendor's pricing page on 8 September 2026. Annual billing is usually cheaper. UNSW-provided tools are listed first. <Badge type="warning" text="TBC" /> marks something we could not confirm on an official page.

## Common to all of them

- **Web app.** Every tool below runs in the browser. A few also have a browser extension, a mobile app, or a Zotero link, noted in the table.
- **Deep research in the general chatbots.** ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all have a mode that searches the web and writes a cited report. ChatGPT calls it deep research and it is included in ChatGPT Edu. Claude calls it Research and it needs a paid plan. Gemini calls it Deep Research and it is free with higher limits on paid plans. Useful for scoping a topic, not a substitute for a systematic search.
- **Verify what they say.** A peer-reviewed evaluation of Elicit found it useful as a second reviewer but not as a replacement for a human. See [Risks and practices](/risks#literature-review-tools-can-miss-or-misextract-data).
