## ChatGPT Edu

Details are in UNSW's [announcement](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2025/09/unsw-sydney-inks-australias-biggest-chatgpt-edu-deal-with-openai) and on [AI@UNSW on MyIT](https://www.unsw.edu.au/myit/emerging-technologies/ai). The points that matter for researchers:

- Sign in with your zID, not a personal account. OpenAI does not train on what you submit.
- The agreement does not override privacy, confidentiality, IP, or ethics rules.
- HDR candidates should talk to their supervisor before using it for their research.
- Codex is part of Edu plans but has to be switched on by UNSW <Badge type="warning" text="TBC" />.

## What UNSW expects in research

*AI and the Responsible Conduct of Research at UNSW* ([PDF](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/unsw-wide/planning-assurance/websites/2026-05-21/ai-and-the-responsible-conduct-of-research-at-unsw.pdf), August 2026) applies to any tool, UNSW-provided or not. In short, though this may be obsolete by the time you read it, so check UNSW's official pages for up-to-date information:

- AI tools cannot be authors or inventors.
- Disclose AI use where it materially contributes to design, data, analysis, code, interpretation, or writing.
- Do not upload restricted data unless you are authorised and safeguards are in place.
- Funder (ARC, NHMRC) and publisher rules on AI still apply.
